PATTERNS

MULTICAM

THE ORIGINAL

The original MultiCam® pattern is the combat-proven solution for concealment when operating in widely varied and mixed terrain. Its unique high-resolution design helps the wearer blend into diverse environments from woodlands to deserts, making it the preferred choice of U.S. Special Operations Forces.

MULTICAM TROPIC

JUNGLE OPTIMIZED

MultiCam® Tropic was developed for use in dense jungle environments. The pattern features a darker, more green-dominant colorway optimized for tropical and heavily vegetated areas where the standard MultiCam® may appear too light.

MULTICAM ARID

DESERT OPTIMIZED

MultiCam® Arid is designed for open, sandy, and rocky desert environments. The lighter, tan-dominant colorway provides superior concealment in arid regions where the original MultiCam® may appear too dark.

MULTICAM ALPINE

SNOW OPTIMIZED

MultiCam® Alpine is optimized for environments that experience significant snowfall. The white-dominant pattern with subtle terrain elements provides effective concealment in alpine and tundra conditions.

MULTICAM BLACK

LAW ENFORCEMENT

MultiCam® Black is designed to offer domestic agencies a distinct and authoritative presence suited to law enforcement operations. The dark colorway provides a professional appearance while maintaining the functional benefits of the MultiCam® pattern family.
