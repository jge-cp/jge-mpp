ABOUT

OUR STORY

MultiCam® is the combat-proven solution for concealment when one needs to operate in widely varied and mixed terrain. Developed through extensive research into natural camouflage and human visual perception, MultiCam® represents a breakthrough in concealment technology.

We spent a lot of time studying how camouflage works in nature, taking into account where it is hardest to hide. We also studied which terrain elements were most common across the widest range of environments. We documented how light affects environmental features and tracked the seasonal and elevational changes in different regions.

Today, MultiCam® is the chosen pattern of U.S. Special Operations Forces and has been adopted by military and law enforcement agencies around the world.

OUR MISSION

To provide maximum concealment effectiveness across diverse operating environments with a minimum logistical burden. Our patterns are designed to work together as a system to meet the needs of nearly any operating environment, helping the wearer do so with the least amount of kit possible.
