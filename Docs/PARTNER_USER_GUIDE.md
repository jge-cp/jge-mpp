# MultiCam Partner Portal - User Guide

This guide is for **Partner** users who submit First Articles (FAs) and Lots through the MultiCam Partner Portal.

---

## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Go to the portal URL provided by your administrator.
2. Enter your **username** and **password**, then click **Login**.
3. You will be taken to your Partner Dashboard.

If you don't have an account, contact your MultiCam administrator to have one created for you.

### Setting Your Password (First-Time Users)

When your account is created, you'll receive a **Welcome** email with a link to set your password. Click the link, choose a password, and you're ready to log in.

### Forgot Your Password?

1. On the login page, click **Forgot your password?**
2. Enter your email address and click **Reset My Password**.
3. Check your email for a reset link.
4. Click the link, set a new password, and log in.

---

## Partner Dashboard

After logging in, you land on the **Partner Dashboard**. This is your home base.

### What You'll See

- **Submit First Article** button -- start a new FA submission.
- **Submit Lot** button -- start a new Lot submission (only available after you have at least one approved FA).
- **All First Articles** -- a searchable, filterable list of your company's FA submissions.
- **All Lots** -- a searchable, filterable list of your company's Lot submissions.

### Filtering and Searching

Both the FA and Lot sections support:

- **Search** -- type a keyword to search by fabric style, lot number, variant, or company.
- **Status** -- filter by Pending, Approved, or Rejected.
- **Variant** -- filter by MultiCam variant (e.g., Multicam, Multicam Black, Multicam Arid).
- **Date range** -- filter by submission date.
- **Sort** -- click column headers to sort.

The dashboard refreshes automatically every 10 seconds so you'll see status updates in real time.

## Submitting a First Article (FA)

A First Article is the initial fabric sample submission for quality review. It goes through a two-stage inspection: **Primary Review** followed by **Final Review**.

### How to Submit

1. From the dashboard, click **Submit First Article**.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked with *):
   - **Fabric Style** -- the name/description of the fabric.
   - **MultiCam Variant** -- which camouflage pattern (e.g., Multicam, Multicam Black, Multicam Tropic).
   - **Shade Standard** -- the shade standard used for printing.
   - **Spectral Reflectance Requirement** -- Alpha, Beta, SWIR, or Visible Spectrum Only.
   - **FA Lot Number** -- your internal lot/PO number for this first article.
   - **Date of Printing** -- when the fabric was printed.
3. Optionally fill in:
   - **Shade Standard Number**
   - **First Article Ship Date**
   - **Tracking Number**
4. **BDCS checkbox** -- check this if the submission is for BDCS, IMTP, or SWIR. These skip Primary Review and go straight to Final Review.
5. **Upload documents** -- attach any supporting files (photos, test reports, etc.).
6. Click **Submit**.

After submission, you'll see a confirmation with your **FA ID** (e.g., ACME-FA-0001) and be taken to the FA detail page.

### What Happens Next

1. **Primary Review** -- a Primary Inspector evaluates pattern execution, shade matching, scale, and spectral reflectance.
2. **Final Review** -- if Primary passes, a Final Inspector performs the second review.
3. **Result** -- the FA is either **Approved** (ready for production lots) or **Rejected** (with comments explaining why).

You'll receive email notifications at each stage.

## Submitting a Lot

Once you have an **approved FA**, you can submit production lots against it for quality acceptance.

### How to Submit

1. From the dashboard, click **Submit Lot**.
2. **Select the approved FA** this lot is based on. The fabric style, variant, shade standard, and spectral reflectance will auto-populate from the FA.
3. Enter:
   - **Number of Yards Printed** -- the yardage for this lot.
   - **Date of Printing**
   - **Date Shipped** (optional)
   - **Tracking Number** (optional)
4. The **number of samples** is calculated automatically based on yardage:
   - Up to 2,000 yards: **2 samples**
   - 2,001 to 10,000 yards: **3 samples**
   - Over 10,000 yards: **5 samples**
5. **Upload documents** if needed.
6. Click **Submit**.

After submission, you'll see a confirmation with your **Lot ID** (e.g., ACME-LOT-0001).

### What Happens Next

Lots go through a single-stage review by the Primary Inspector. You'll receive an email notification when the review is complete with the result (Approved or Rejected).

## Viewing Submission Details

### FA Detail Page

Click on any FA from the dashboard or the FA list to see:

- **Status** -- Pending (Primary Review), Pending (Final Review), Approved, or Rejected.
- **Submission details** -- fabric style, variant, lot number, shade standard, spectral reflectance, dates.
- **Evaluation results** (once reviewed) -- the inspector's findings for pattern execution, shade matching, scale, spectral reflectance, and color evaluations.
- **Resubmit** -- if a FA is rejected, you can resubmit it for another round of review.
- **Evaluation History** -- view all past evaluations for this FA.

### Lot Detail Page

Click on any Lot from the dashboard or the Lot list to see:

- **Status** -- Pending, Approved, or Rejected.
- **Lot details** -- fabric style, variant, lot number, yards printed, sample count, link to the original FA.
- **Sample numbers** -- the individual sample IDs for this lot.
- **Evaluation results** (once reviewed) -- per-sample pass/fail results with detailed breakdowns.

## Resubmitting a Rejected FA

If your FA is rejected, you can resubmit it:

1. Go to the FA detail page.
2. Click **Resubmit for Review**.
3. The FA returns to the review queue for another round of evaluation.

This is useful after you've made corrections to address the inspector's feedback.

## File Repository

Your administrator may upload reference files (PDFs, images, specifications) that are relevant to your company.

1. In the sidebar, click **Files** under Resources.
2. Browse available files. Depending on your company's categories (Standard, Narrow, or both), you'll see the relevant files.
3. Click a file to view it directly in your browser.

## Notifications

The portal sends email notifications for key events:

- **FA Submitted** -- confirmation that your FA was received.
- **FA Primary Review Complete** -- result of the first review stage.
- **FA Final Review Complete** -- result of the second review stage (Approved or Rejected).
- **Lot Submitted** -- confirmation that your lot was received.
- **Lot Review Complete** -- result of the lot review (Approved or Rejected).

You can also see notifications in the portal via the **bell icon** in the top navigation bar.

## Account Settings

Click **Settings** in the sidebar to view your account information:

- Name, email, username
- Role and account status
- Company details
- Contact information

Account settings are read-only. Contact your administrator to make changes.
